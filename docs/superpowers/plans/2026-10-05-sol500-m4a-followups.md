# SOL 500 M4a — follow-ups for later milestones

M4a (exploring: finds, the backpack, suit oxygen and the rover's battery) is built on `feat/m4a-expedition-core` as version 3.3.0, with 278 tests passing. ### Rules and co-op sync (the code M4b changes next)
- `driving()` accepts an undefined crew id (`src/expedition.js:67`): `park(m, undefined, pos)` moves a parked rover and `setRoverDriven(m, undefined, 399)` drains it. `park` also ignores `m.status` (`:233`), so a stray park after the mission ends moves the rover. Fix: `!!id &&` in `driving()` and a status check in `park`.
- `setSuit` (`src/expedition.js:254`) turns `''`, `false`, `[]` and `null` into 0, which empties the suit instead of ignoring junk, and the server's guard `body.suit != null` (`lib/rooms.js:98`) lets them through (`driven` at `:99` too, harmlessly: it only rises and is capped). One `typeof === 'number'` check in `setSuit` fixes both.
- `inBaseZone` is closed at +192 (`src/expedition.js:62-63`) while the plan's constraint says −128..191 (x = 192 is tile 3); the zone-edge check stops at x = 191.8. Shelters reuse the zone, so settle which is right then.
- `expeditionAction`'s `default:` runs `park` (`lib/rooms.js:42`): an action added to `EXPEDITION_ACTIONS` (`:24`) without a case silently parks. Make `park` explicit and `default` return `{ error: 'badAction' }`, before M4b adds its first new action.
- A heartbeat in flight during a revive can set the server's suit to 0 again (the client adopts `X.suitOf` on the down → up change at `src/surface-eva.js:153`, and its next send restores 30 within about 110 ms plus latency). Harden it by not sending the suit while down.
- A click-to-walk path survives refused steps (`src/surface.js:493-525`): a collapsed co-op player keeps `moving: true` (sent to teammates) and resumes the old walk after a revive with no input. Clear `p.path` when both axes are refused.
- Exploring state outlives a mission: `SolSurface.reset()` (`src/surface.js:84-88`) leaves `S.eva` alone and `sync` resets only on a turn or crew change (`src/surface-eva.js:141`), so a new mission or room at turn 0 with the same astronaut inherits the old suit and `driven` (it only rises, `:156`), and the next `suit()` report writes them into the new mission. Fix: an `S.eva.reset()` that clears `turn` and `me`, called from `reset()`. The final review weighs this first.
- `park` is sent once, fire and forget (`src/surface.js:755`; `src/surface-eva.js:29` says quiet actions are "retried or harmless"): a transport failure leaves `x.rover = { driver }` on the server (`position()` never clears it), so teammates get `taken` until the round advances or the driver boards or parks again.
- Tests for the rules and rooms: the `canister` and `pickup` actions are never sent through `act()` and `load` is only asserted as 0 (`test/rooms.test.js:213`); no test shows that a refused action doesn't broadcast; the suit test waits 150 ms against the 80 ms throttle (the file's existing pattern).
- Tests for the exploring layer (`test/surface-eva.test.js`): the keep-going re-hold (`src/surface-eva.js:219`) and the revive and pickup hold paths are untested, only the find hold is covered; the `id !== me` half of the fresh-look trigger (`:141`) is untested (pin it with a takeover sync: controlId `diego`, `suits.diego = 40` → suit 40); deleting `:147` (the driven reset on a new look) survives every test (pin it: `roverDriven = 100` → `resolveTurn` → `sync(next)` → `report().driven === 0`); `:150` is equivalent to `false` because `:154` re-syncs it; no test reads `eva.driving()` or `roverAtBay()` after a reload; `:140` hard-codes the role `'biologist'` for the hold time (read minh's role from the mission); the comment at `:88-89` misstates the race (the server ignores suit reports from a collapsed crew member, `X.setSuit` returns early at `src/expedition.js:256`; the hazard is the client's own stale local 0, re-sent after the revive); the titles at `:74` and `:149` no longer describe everything they cover, 'with an empty suit' is reused at `:96`, `:169` and `:172`, and the old title "a new round means a full suit again, and says so" never asserts the note.
- The browser checks exercise only successes per hook. Task 8 added real refusals (`taken`, `collapsed`, `notAtBase`); `full`, `tooFar`, `flat`, `empty` and `noRover` are still checked only in unit tests. If the +27 px host nudge seen once in Task 7 recurs, add `keys`, `moving` and `path` to `probe()`.

### From the Task 8 QA
- **Reload while collapsed (co-op, narrow).** The server keeps a collapsed seat at the spot where the suit ran out, but a reloaded tab builds its avatar at the HQ spawn. Observed: after the reload the avatar stands at (32, 96) and teammates see it there with suit 0, while `collapsed.minh` is still (352, 1184), where the revive prompt appears; the client then re-sends `deliver` every 1.5 s because it is "at the base" with a pack, and the server refuses each with `collapsed` (5 refused requests in 8 s, 60 in the first run). After a revive the player would carry on from the HQ. Fix: on a fresh look at a collapsed seat, place the avatar at `collapsed[me]` (`ensurePlayer` and `moveToCrewmate`, `src/surface.js`), and skip the deliver retry while `collapsed(me)` (`src/surface-eva.js:190`). M4b's shelters make a collapse inside a shelter possible, so a guard there matters more.
- **The canister adds to a stale suit level.** `X.useCanister` adds 45 s to the suit level the layer last reported to the mission (every 2 s, `src/surface-eva.js:199`), not to the live level, and the layer adopts the answer (`:129`). Observed: live suit 77.4 s, last report 78.6 s, after Q 123.2 s (78.6 + 45 less 0.4 s). The error is at most 2 s of drain (4 s sprinting). Fix: send the live suit with the `canister` action, or add the 45 s on the client. M4b's O₂ ruptures also change the suit between reports.
- **The saved rover battery lags the live one in solo.** `suit()` sends `driven` only while you are in the rover (`src/surface-eva.js:202`) and `park` carries no distance, so the last up-to-2 s of driving is never saved. Observed: 24.4 tiles driven, 15.0 saved after getting out, and the same after a reload (9.4 tiles of battery regained). Fix: send the distance with `park` (and on a collapse) or once more when you get out.

### Worldgen and the shared world cache
- `findById` builds the chunk an unvalidated id names before the position check (`src/expedition.js:47`): 1,000 far ids cost about 200 ms and evict other rooms' chunks from the shared 256-chunk cache (`:27`). Fix: in `collect`, refuse an id whose chunk isn't the one containing `pos`. M4c adds more checked actions on the same cache, so do this first.
- `findsIn` returns the cache's own unfrozen arrays (`src/expedition.js:38`): a consumer that annotates a find corrupts every mission with that seed. Freeze in `chunkAt` or document it as read-only; structures and landmarks will read the same chunks.
- The ice-spot loop doesn't apply `FIND_GAP` (`src/worldgen.js:347-352`); the 5-tile gap holds there only because features are spaced. Add the `out.some` filter or a comment.
- The region nibble decode is repeated (`src/worldgen.js:351`, `:360`, `regionOf` at `:405`), and the salts 500, 540 and 580 overlap if `FIND_DARTS` grows past 40.
- No direct test that canyon middles hold ice (117 of 117 are right today); mirror the crater assertion in `test/worldgen-finds.test.js`.
- About 24 % of craters (474 of 1,983 measured) have no ice find on their floor, because ice goes only in crater centres and canyon middles that a chunk owns (3 tiles inside). Spec §2.4 only says "crater floors, canyons, Icy Plains", which holds. Clamping would move the pinned finds hash (`0e84f5a1`) and the balance tuned on it.

### The final rebalance
- **The sloppy-bot target (spec §1.4):** exploring should raise the "sloppy player, recommended kit" bot's success by 10–25 points. Measured: cadet 100.0 → 100.0 % (+0.0), commander 98.1 → 100.0 % (+1.9), veteran 66.8 → 97.5 % (+30.7); on commander with the rover kit 15.0 → 90.8 % (+75.8). The bot is already at about 100 % on cadet and commander, so +10–25 can't show there; veteran overshoots, and the rover kit overshoots by far. Decide in the rebalance whether the target is restated (for example, measured on veteran) or the explorer's haul is cut.
- **The hard targets hold, with little room on one:** careless + explorer 31.3 / 9.9 / 1.2 % (target under 35 %; the cadet margin is 3.7 points, so watch it) and thoughtful + explorer 100 / 100 / 100 % (over 70 %). The haul plateaus: 8.8 → 3.0 loads a round on foot and 18.9 → 7.6 with the rover.
- **Grade inflation:** with exploring almost every win is an S. Commander, per 1,000 runs: sloppy A 815 / B 102 / S 64 / D 19 becomes S 913 / A 86 / B 1; thoughtful A 895 / S 84 / B 20 / D 1 becomes S 981 / A 19; with the rover kit and the explorer, thoughtful scores 2,373 on average (S 999 of 1,000). The average score goes from 1,254 to 1,646 (sloppy) and from 1,324 to 1,736 (thoughtful). The grade bands (`D.SCORE.grades`, `src/data.js:228`: S 1,400, A 1,200, B 1,000, C 800) were set before exploring, so they need new bands or a different weight for science.
- A re-roll of the dart draws has about a 4 % chance to trip the ring-1 density limit, and the finds tests are calibrated on today's draws: the limits 0.6 and 0.75 (`test/worldgen-finds.test.js:127-128`) sit on the fixed RING-0..39 seeds, so re-measure; test 1's "this chunk has finds to compare" (`:27`) depends on chunk (3, −2) of `FIND-ORDER` holding a find; both pins sit in one test, so after an intended change the first failing assert hides the second hash; the pin comment at `:147` says the old sample's chunks are all ring 3, but it is the finds that are (chunk (0, 0) is in the sample, empty).
