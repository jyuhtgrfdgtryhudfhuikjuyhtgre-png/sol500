# SOL 500 M6 balance (Task 8 part 1: ranks, the Garage, the Fabricator, gear, Colony)

**Status:** done. Every target met at 300 runs and confirmed at 1000.
**Branch:** `m6-t8` off `130b8fa`. **Before:** 3.7.0 (`d6a3cb9`). Part 1 leaves out the pressurised rover and the drone (ruling R18).

All runs: `node tools/balance.js <runs>` (seeds `BAL-0..`), Full mission, 4 crew, the level's reference kit. `LEVELS=commander,veteran` picks the levels (there is no difficulty argument; the default runs cadet, commander and veteran). `130b8fa` before any Task 8 change printed exactly 3.7.0's output (`cmp`: identical), because no bot bought anything M6 yet.

## What the bots do now (tools/balance.js)

- **Thoughtful** (`smartPlan`): buys, one at a time and keeping a 300 kg reserve, the first of: the next rank (at Colony the launch pad, then the launch), a Garage, a Fabricator, gear (tanks, backpack, racks only when the base has a rover, tools). A tanks level is skipped when the forecast says it would take the water the crew drinks. These come after a relay kit and before a shield; the engineer finishes a growth build before going back to maintenance. Played without exploring it buys only the ranks (the rest helps nobody who stays home).
- **Sloppy** (`sloppyPlan`): ranks up **once, late**: in the mission's second half, to Outpost, when the store covers the cost and one repair (100 kg) besides. It builds nothing there. (Tried first: "once, as soon as it can": with a 500 kg Outpost it spent the starting materials in round 1 and lost the jury-rig repairs, so sloppy without exploring fell from 70 % to 13.7 % success on veteran. A sloppy player is careless with priorities, not suicidal.)
- **Careless** (`naivePlan`): never builds, so never ranks up (spec: "a careless player stays a Camp or an Outpost"; the brief: never).
- `ponytail:` the bot doesn't build the extra solar/greenhouses a bigger base allows, so independence from more buildings is under-measured.
- The explorer (`tools/explorer.js`) already read gear through `X.suitMax`, `X.capacity` (`packOf`/`rackOf`) and `X.holdSeconds(…, m)`, so it is unchanged. It does not model the base zone (the suit drains from the HQ's door at every rank), so a bigger zone's shorter trips are not measured.

## Why the prices had to fall

The materials a thoughtful explorer brings in (median of 100 commander runs, cumulative, start stock included): 660 kg by round 1, 1,422 by 5, 2,290 by 10, 3,022 by 15, 3,864 by 20 — about 180 kg a round. Its shields and relay kits take 900 kg of that and most of the engineer's time. With the starting prices (ranks 800 / 1,600 / 2,800) it reached Outpost in round 10 (median) and Colony never. Gear compounds: level-1 gear from round 1 would raise the income to ~300 kg a round, level 2 to ~370, but the Fabricator only comes at Station.
