# SOL 500 M4c — follow-ups for later milestones

M4c (landmarks with discovery cards, Kestrel Survey structures, natural formations, and lava tubes and hab pods as cover) is built on `feat/m4c-points-of-interest` as version 3.5.0. 472 tests pass at its last code change, `9dd28e5` (470 in the plan's dry run, 354 at M4b). Tasks 1–4 and 6–9 passed their reviews with no Important finding. Task 5 and Task 10 each needed one fix round: Task 5's lookup of a client's landmark or structure id, copied in four to six places at the server's trust boundary, became one helper (`5a77f1d`), and Task 10's two Important findings (every plain collect said "You revived …", and the HUD column covered the module menu) were fixed in `aba1a15`, with a regression test for the first. Task 10's browser check then found the top bar wrapping to two rows beside the forecast chip, fixed in `97d0cfb`. The final whole-branch review (`3e634a7..aba1a15`) found nothing Critical or Important and called the branch ready to release after Task 11; of its two Minors, the survey spot's comment and a guard test are in `9dd28e5`, and the lost card is under the M4d plan below. Task 11's browser QA on `9dd28e5` passed all 9 of its checks, with one Minor left for M5. Line numbers in this doc are those of `aba1a15`.

The task reviews deferred 47 minor findings. Two were closed by later work: a stale `dist/` that the rebuild in the release commit replaces, and `worldgen.js` comments that became true when Task 3 landed. Three are design calls for the user (Task 8's look, last section). The other 42 are below, merged where several reviews flagged the same thing, together with the notes the controller and the reviews made along the way and the plan's own list of items for later plans.

This doc holds everything the reviews and the QA deferred: pick the items up in the milestone named. The task reports, the saved reviews and the build ledger it draws on live in the git-ignored workspace `.superpowers/sdd/2026-10-21-sol500-m4c-points-of-interest/`, not in the repository. The M4c plan's header lists the items it took from the M4a and M4b follow-ups docs (stable find ids, landmarks, cards and lava-tube interiors, hab pods, formations with sourced facts, and the one cover lister); everything else in those docs stays there, except the M4b doc's `npm run smoke` item, fixed before the 3.5.0 release: the smoke now runs the app in an empty profile of its own and deletes it after (`tools/app-smoke.js`), and a run wrote nothing to the player's real profile.
