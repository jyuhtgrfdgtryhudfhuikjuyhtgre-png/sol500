# SOL 500 M4b — Dangers Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Make going outside dangerous, and make the danger readable.
- In a round whose schedule has a solar particle event, a **solar flare's warning** starts 30 s after the first crew member steps outside, and a 60 s countdown follows. Cover is the base zone, a cave's mouth or the built storm shelter. Whoever is out in the open when it hits takes +15 mSv at the advance.
- **Caves** open in the south faces of mesas and canyon walls, about three times as many in the Volcanic Flanks.
- **Dust devils** wander on a clock every screen shares. One that catches you on foot throws you 2 tiles and costs 10 s of suit; the rover is immune.
- **Dust storms** shrink how far you see: 8 tiles in clear weather (10 in the rover), 5 in a regional storm, 3 in a global one.
- In co-op the server times the flare and judges cover from the positions it last saw.

**Architecture:**
- **`src/worldgen.js`** places caves in every data chunk, after the finds, from the chunk's own tiles (pure and deterministic, like the terrain).
- **`src/dangers.js`** (new, pure UMD, `SolDangers`) holds the danger rules:
  - how far you can see; what covers you; the flare's start and its hit; the solo round clock; the dust devils.
  - `ui.js` runs the flare in solo play and `lib/rooms.js` in co-op, so both play by the same rules.
  - Every screen computes the dust devils itself from the mission code, the round and the round's clock: nothing is synced.
- **`src/engine.js`** keeps the new expedition fields (`flare`, `clock`, `where`), repairs them in old saves, charges +15 mSv for a `flare` incident at the advance and resets them when the round rolls over.
- **`src/expedition.js`** takes the M4a follow-up fixes: the canister guard, the half-open base zone, the driver's id, typed setters, the rover distance on park and collapse, and where you stand (for a reload).
- **`lib/rooms.js`** sends the round's clock with every room frame, starts and times the flare, and parks a rover whose driver says they got out.
- **`src/surface-eva.js`** (with `surface.js`, `surface-paint.js`, `surface-world.js`, `surface-map.js` and `style.css`) shows it all: the flare's banner, the red edge and the flash, the cover rings, the dust devils and their throw, the storm view, cave mouths.
- **`tools/explorer.js`** pays the dangers' toll in the balance runs.

**Tech Stack:** Plain browser JavaScript (no bundler; UMD/IIFE modules on `window`), Canvas 2D, Node 24 built-in test runner (`node --test`), `ws` (already a dependency), Electron (unchanged).

## Rulings made while planning (binding unless the user overrules them)

1. **Where caves open.**
   - A cave's mouth is the open tile below a south-facing mesa cliff or canyon wall: the face the surface draws, so you can see it.
   - Each such wall tile opens a cave with chance 0.3 (1 in the Volcanic Flanks), at least 6 tiles from the chunk's other caves either way (2 in the Volcanic Flanks). That gives the spec's "caves ×3" there: measured 3.2× the Plains.
   - Lava tubes, and their interiors as shelter, come with M4c (spec §2.2 and §3.4); so do abandoned hab pods.
2. **What counts as cover.**
   - The base zone, the tile within 0.9 tiles of a cave mouth's middle, and the same around the built storm shelter (its tile on the build grid; the grid reaches 4 tiles from the HQ).
   - Co-op judges with the 1.5 tiles of slack every checked action gets; solo with none.
3. **The round's clock.** The dust devils move by it and the flare is timed by it.
   - **Solo:** the surface's own time. It stops while the surface is hidden (like the suit), and is saved with the mission every 2 s, so a reload picks it up.
   - **Co-op:** the server's: seconds since the round began, sent with every room frame. Each tab runs it on from when the frame arrived.
4. **Who starts the flare.** The first crew member to step out of the base zone in a round whose schedule has `spe` (spec §3.4).
   - **Co-op:** the server, on the first such position, from a crew member who is active and not collapsed.
   - **Solo:** the surface asks for `flareStart` with the round's clock.
5. **Who the flare hits.** Anyone out of cover when the countdown ends.
   - **Co-op:** by the positions the server last saw. Crew without a player are at the base, under cover.
   - **Solo:** the pilot, unless they are sitting out the round after a collapse (they are at the base).
   - A round that ends before the hit calls the flare off.
   - The hit is a `flare` incident: +15 mSv at the advance, no health and no morale. The forecast shows it as soon as it is recorded.
6. **Dust devils per data chunk, not per explorer** (needs the user's OK, spec §13.10).
   - The spec says "3–5 devils near each explorer". Here a dust-devil round has 3–5 devils a 32-tile chunk at any time (other rounds 0–1, twice that in the Dune Sea), and none within 16 tiles of the HQ.
   - Why: every screen draws the same devils from the mission code, the round and the clock, without knowing where anyone is.
   - Measured: about 1.7 devils within 12 tiles of you in a dust-devil round (0.23 in other rounds); a walker who never dodges is hit 1.61 times in a 150 s EVA (0.18 in other rounds).
   - A devil lives 24 s and drifts 6–12 tiles with the round's wind, give or take 45°. It hits when its middle passes within 0.55 tiles of your feet once it has formed. The throw is a 0.25 s slide that stops at walls. 2 s of calm follow a hit.
7. **The view in a storm** is the spec's 8/10/5/3. In a storm the rover sees as far as you do on foot. Dust devils fade out past the edge of your view, so in a storm one can be on you before you see it.
8. **Showing the flare.**
   - A banner at the top centre, in the hint's place: what to do and the seconds left; green while you have cover. On phones it is the strip at 150 px, over the region card.
   - A red edge that grows and beats faster as the countdown runs out (only while you are in the open), and a flash when it hits.
   - Every cover in view pulses amber during the warning: the base zone, cave mouths, the storm shelter.
   - The verdict stays on the banner for 8 s. A note when the warning starts, and a once-a-round brief of what is outside.
   - The Expedition panel lists what is outside this round; the report has a flare line; help item 11 explains the dangers.
9. **The O₂ rupture needs no new rule.** M4a's bank already adds 30 kg of O₂ for each canister carried home, on top of the leak. M4b says so in the panel and the brief, and pins it with an engine test.
10. **`index.html` and `sw.js` load and cache `src/dangers.js` in Task 3**, with the module. `src/surface-eva.js` already runs in the page, so the page must have `SolDangers` before Task 6 changes the layer to use it.
11. **The banner's CSS** came out of the dry run's browser QA. A box at `left: 50%` only gets the right half of the stage to wrap in, so the banner is `width: max-content` up to its `max-width`. The seconds never wrap. The background is as solid as the region card's, which the phone banner covers.
12. **The M4a ruling on trust stands.** Co-op still trusts the suit level, the rover distance and the moment of a collapse that a player's game reports.

  - `chunk(seed, cx, cy)` also returns `caves: [{ n, x, y, region }]`.
    - `x` and `y` are world tiles: the cave's **mouth**, an open tile. Its wall (a mesa cliff or a canyon wall) is the tile north of it, in the same chunk.
    - `n` is the index; a cave's id would be `"<cx>,<cy>:<n>"` (nothing stores one yet).
    - `region` indexes `REGIONS` (the mouth's region).
  - Rules: chance 0.3 per south-facing wall tile (1 in the Volcanic Flanks); at least 6 tiles from the chunk's other caves either way (2 in the Volcanic Flanks); never in the flat zone (it has no walls); never on the base.

'use strict';
// Caves in the endless world (src/worldgen.js, spec §3.1 and §3.4, M4b): where their mouths open, how many there are in
// each region, and that adding them moved nothing else.
const test = require('node:test');
const assert = require('node:assert/strict');
const W = require('../src/worldgen.js');

const T = W.TILE;
const REGION = Object.fromEntries(W.REGIONS.map((r, i) => [r, i]));
const inFlat = (x, y) => x * x + y * y <= (W.FLAT_RADIUS + 1) * (W.FLAT_RADIUS + 1);

test('a cave opens in the south face of a mesa or a canyon wall: its mouth is the open tile below the wall, inside its chunk', () => {
  let n = 0;
  for (const code of ['CAVE-1', 'CAVE-2', 'CAVE-3', 'CAVE-4']) {
    const seed = W.seedOf(code);
    for (let cy = -5; cy < 5; cy++) for (let cx = -5; cx < 5; cx++) {
      const c = W.chunk(seed, cx, cy);
      c.caves.forEach((k, i) => {
        n++;
        assert.equal(k.n, i, 'n is the index');
        const lx = k.x - cx * 32, ly = k.y - cy * 32;
        assert.ok(lx >= 0 && lx < 32 && ly >= 1 && ly < 32, `cave ${cx},${cy}:${i} has its mouth and its wall in its chunk`);
        const mouth = W.tileOf(c, lx, ly), wall = W.tileOf(c, lx, ly - 1);
        assert.equal(W.BLOCKING[mouth], 0, 'the mouth is open ground');
        assert.notEqual(mouth, T.BASE);
        assert.ok(wall === T.CLIFF || wall === T.CANYON_WALL, 'the wall is a mesa cliff or a canyon wall, not ' + wall);
        assert.equal(k.region, W.regionOf(c, lx, ly), "its region is its mouth's");
        assert.ok(!inFlat(k.x, k.y), 'never in the flat zone round the base');
      });
    }
  }
  assert.ok(n > 40, 'caves to check: ' + n);
});

test('the same chunk always has the same caves, whatever was built before it', () => {

test('caves keep apart: 6 tiles either way, 2 in the Volcanic Flanks, which have about three times as many', () => {
  const caves = [0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0], tiles = [0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0];
  for (let s = 0; s < 20; s++) {
    const seed = W.seedOf('CFAR-' + s);
    for (let i = 0; i < 400; i++) {
      const c = W.chunk(seed, (i % 20) * 9 - 90, Math.floor(i / 20) * 9 - 90);
      for (let j = 0; j < 1024; j++) tiles[W.regionOf(c, j % 32, j >> 5)]++;
      for (const a of c.caves) {
        caves[a.region]++;
        for (const b of c.caves) {
          if (a === b) continue;
          const gap = Math.max(Math.abs(a.x - b.x), Math.abs(a.y - b.y));
          assert.ok(gap >= 2, 'caves ' + a.n + ' and ' + b.n + ' are ' + gap + ' tiles apart');
          if (a.region !== REGION.volcanic && b.region !== REGION.volcanic) assert.ok(gap >= 6, 'outside the Volcanic Flanks caves keep 6 tiles apart');
        }
      }
    }
  }
  const per = r => caves[r] / tiles[r] * 1024;
  // Measured: 0.80 caves a chunk in the Volcanic Flanks, 0.25 in the Plains and 0.74 in the Canyon Lands, whose walls run
  // for many tiles; the Dune Sea, the Crater Field and the Icy Plains have few walls (0.08–0.11).
  const k = per(REGION.volcanic) / per(REGION.plains);
  assert.ok(k > 2.4 && k < 4.5, 'the Volcanic Flanks have ' + k.toFixed(2) + ' times the caves of the Plains');
  assert.ok(per(REGION.canyons) > per(REGION.plains), 'canyon walls hold more caves than the Plains');
});

// FNV-1a over a JSON string.
function fnv(text) {

}

test('a devil forms, drifts 6–12 tiles with the wind and dies down within 24 s', () => {
  const m = solo('DNG-DRIFT', { devil: true });
  const seen = new Map();
  for (let t = 0; t <= 200; t += 0.5) {
    for (const d of devilsAround(m, 160, 160, 100, t)) {
      const s = seen.get(d.id) || { first: t, last: t, x0: d.x, y0: d.y, x1: d.x, y1: d.y, max: 0 };
      s.last = t; s.x1 = d.x; s.y1 = d.y; s.max = Math.max(s.max, d.a);
      seen.set(d.id, s);
    }
  }
  assert.ok(seen.size > 20, 'devils seen: ' + seen.size);
  let whole = 0;
  for (const [id, s] of seen) {
    assert.ok(s.last - s.first < DV.life, id + ' lasts ' + (s.last - s.first) + ' s');
    const [cx, cy] = id.split(':')[0].split(',').map(Number);
    if (cx < 3 || cx > 6 || cy < 3 || cy > 6) continue; // a chunk near the box's edge: its devils drift in and out of view
    if (s.first < 0.5 || s.last > 199.5) continue; // cut off by the sample's start or end
    whole++;
    const moved = Math.hypot(s.x1 - s.x0, s.y1 - s.y0) / T, seen0 = (s.last - s.first) / DV.life;
    assert.ok(moved <= DV.track[1] * seen0 + 0.01, id + ' drifted ' + moved.toFixed(2) + ' tiles');
    assert.ok(moved >= DV.track[0] * seen0 - 0.01, id + ' drifted only ' + moved.toFixed(2) + ' tiles');
    assert.equal(s.max, 1, id + ' formed fully');
  }
  assert.ok(whole > 10, 'devils seen from start to end: ' + whole);
});

// FNV-1a over a JSON string.
function fnv(text) {
  let h = 2166136261;
  for (let i = 0; i < text.length; i++) h = Math.imul(h ^ text.charCodeAt(i), 16777619);
  return (h >>> 0).toString(16).padStart(8, '0');
}
