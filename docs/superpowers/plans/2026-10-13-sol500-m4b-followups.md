## The Display settings (Task G)
The user asked for this on 2026-10-14, in the middle of M4b: "add the fps option and put the graphic on its best". Asked to choose, they picked both FPS controls (a Show FPS toggle and a 30 / 60 / Max limit) and a Graphics setting that defaults to Best, then approved the design in chat ("yes"). There is no spec section or plan for it: spec §12 has one ticked line, and this section is its record. It ships in 3.4.0. The source is the Task G brief and report (`.superpowers/sdd/2026-10-13-sol500-m4b-dangers/task-G-brief.md` and `task-G-report.md`), which live in the git-ignored workspace, not in the repository.
- **Where:** a Display button (a gear and the word) in the top bar after Achievements (`src/ui.js:353`) and an icon-only gear in the operations header (`src/ui.js:938`). Both open the dialog (`displayModal()`, `src/ui.js:1755-1766`): Graphics (Best, Fast, with the hint of the chosen level), FPS limit (30, 60, Max, one hint) and Show FPS (Off, On). A choice saves at once; there is no OK button.
- **Best** (the default): the canvas at `min(devicePixelRatio, 4)` (it was `min(…, 2)`), ground image chunks painted at 2× (512 px square in place of 256) and dust × 1.5 (39, 165 and 330 grains in clear weather, a regional storm and a global storm). **Fast:** ratio 1, chunks at 1×, dust × 0.5 (13, 55 and 110). The big map and the minimap keep their own ratio. Changing the level while playing resizes the canvas and repaints the ground at once, with no reload (`src/surface.js:194-204` and `:369-377`; `setScale` in `src/surface-paint.js`).
- **The FPS limit** is 30, 60 or Max (0, every callback). The loop keeps `requestAnimationFrame` running and skips callbacks until the next draw is due, with 1 ms of tolerance, so a limit of 60 on a 144 Hz screen averages 60 (`src/surface.js:444-445`). A skipped callback leaves the game's clock alone, so the game plays at real time; the camera's ease and the dust's drift now use `dt` too (`33ed5f1`, `src/surface.js:1010` and `:1146-1147`).
- **The readout** (`.sf-fps`) sits under the minimap (`src/style.css:529`; 128 px down on phones, `:542`), refreshes at most 4 times a second, is `aria-hidden` and is hidden when Show FPS is off.
- **Stored per device** in `localStorage['sol500.settings']` as `gfx: 'best' | 'fast'`, `fpsCap: 0 | 30 | 60` (0 is Max) and `showFps: boolean` (defaults at `src/ui.js:24`); never sent to a co-op server. A stored value outside those sets reads as the default (`src/ui.js:54`).
- English and Vietnamese strings (14 keys; `common.on` and `common.off` are reused), the gear icon (`src/art.js:52`) and 9 tests. `sw.js` needed no change: its cache `sol500-v4` was already bumped for 3.4.0 (`sol500-v3` on `master`) and every changed file is in `SHELL`.

### Shelters and the cover rules (do these first)

Fix: `Z.coversIn(m, x0, y0, x1, y1)`, the cover spots in a box, used by `shelterAt`, the rings and the surface's checks.
- **Feed it from the surface's own chunks.** The surface asks `Z.shelterAt` every frame while you are outside the base zone (`src/surface-eva.js:121`) and, through `covered()`, during a countdown (`:105`, `:467`). - **The slack in one place.** It is written in `lib/rooms.js:150` (`D.EXPEDITION.reach`, 1.5 tiles), `src/ui.js:753` (0, solo) and `src/surface-eva.js:96` (`coop ? reach : 0`). They agree today. Fix: the lister owns it.

- **The shelters themselves:** lava tubes and their interiors, and abandoned hab pods, join `shelterAt`'s list. Lava-tube headlamps aren't affected by storms (spec §3.4): `viewTiles` (`src/dangers.js:36`) cuts every view by the weather, so a headlamp must not read it.

### Co-op timing and the rover
- **Tag positions with the round.** A position sent just before an advance and still in flight lands in the new round and starts its flare at clock 0 (`lib/rooms.js:116-120`; the advance clears `room.positions` at `:224`, which doesn't cover a message still on its way). At worst it makes a round easier: the flare passes while everyone plans at the base. Fix: the client tags each position with its round, and the server ignores other rounds.
- **The flare timer drops `flareEnd`'s `early` error** (`lib/rooms.js:150`; `src/dangers.js:86` answers `early` when the clock reads more than 0.5 s before the end). It needs the host's wall clock to step back during a countdown (`age()`, `lib/rooms.js:77`, is `Date.now()`); the flare then never lands and co-op banners stay on "The solar flare is here" until the advance. Fix: on `early`, call `flareLater(room)` again (one line), or time the countdown with a monotonic clock. This and the item above share a root, Date-based and stale co-op timing.
- **The stale-position guard gives up recovery in three rarer races** (`lib/rooms.js:113-114` parks only when the stored position says `rover: true`). (a) A driver boards and gets out within about 110 ms and the `park` is lost, or reaches the server before the `board`. (b) A rejoin re-claims the seat after a lost `park` (`claim` deletes the stored position, `:277-278`). (c) Getting out and straight back in: an on-foot position sent just before the second board lands after it, which is the original race in a narrower window. After (a) or (b) the server keeps you as the driver until the advance (`src/engine.js:1003`): the rover is drawn nowhere, nobody can board it and your pack shows 8 slots. Each needs two rare events together. Fix: an epoch on positions (the client's board count, or the round as above), acting only on a newer one.
- **A co-op body's server anchor trails the drawn body.** The server anchors a collapse at the last position it received over the WebSocket (`lib/rooms.js:449`), at most every 110 ms, so it can trail the stopped body by up to 110 ms of slide: about 51–56 px at 60 fps, about 77 px at worst. - **Tests for the rooms, paths nothing pins.** Removing any of these stays green: `collapse` and `park` forwarding `driven` (`lib/rooms.js:41`, `:44`), the "active, not collapsed" clause of the flare starter (`:116-117`) and the `stopFlare` calls (`:210`, `:223`, `:315`, `:360`; the second guard behind the turn check). ### The Display settings (Task G review)
- **Fast on a zoomed-out browser.** Fast is a flat ratio of 1 (`src/surface.js:197`). With the browser zoomed out (Ctrl and minus, `devicePixelRatio` 0.5–0.9) it draws a canvas with 1.2–4× the pixels of Best's `min(4, dpr)`, so "fewer pixels" is reversed. Fix: `Math.min(1, window.devicePixelRatio || 1)` for Fast, and a ratio-0.5 line in the pixel-ratio test (`test/surface.test.js:518`).
- **A window drag can show two blank frames under a limit.** `S.nextDraw = 0` (`src/surface.js:376`) covers a quality change only. `resize()` also runs from the ResizeObserver and clears the canvas the same way, and under a limit the next callback can be skipped, so a drag at a limit of 30 can present 2 blank frames instead of 1. Fix: move the line into `resize()` (`:195`) and drop it from `applyGfx`; do it with the M4c test fix above if those tests are open.
- **The painter stub accepts any method name.** `stubPainter` (`test/surface.test.js:58-62`) is a Proxy, so a misspelt surface-to-painter call (`setscale`, `retainview`) can't fail the surface suite; only the real painter's own tests know the names. Fix: build the stub from `Object.keys(P.createPainter(...))`, or throw on an unknown name.
- **The Display rows have no accessible name.** `seg()` (`src/ui.js:116`) makes a `role="group"` whose label is a sibling `span.eyebrow`; the Display rows (`:1759-1764`) and the setup rows (`:1576-1581`) share the pattern. Fix: an `aria-label` (or `aria-labelledby`) from the row's label inside `seg()`.
- **The ground's scale isn't shown.** `stats()` (`src/surface-paint.js:365`) and the debug box (`debugText`, `src/surface-map.js:204`) don't say which scale the ground is painted at, so in a page the only evidence of 2× chunks is the canvas size and the look. Fix: add `scale` to `stats()` and print one token in the debug box.
- **If Best is too heavy on some machine,** the dials, cheapest first: (1) `groundScale()` to 1.5 at Best (`src/surface.js:194`: 384 px images, 2.25× the memory and fill in place of 4×; `PX * scale` stays an integer, and the surface test's `seen.scale === 2` changes); (2) zero evicted canvases, `c.width = c.height = 0` where images are dropped (`retainView` and `setScale` in `src/surface-paint.js`), if a trace shows spikes every few seconds while driving; (3) cap Best's ratio at 2 above a `devicePixelRatio` of 2. The numbers behind them are estimates from the code: an image is 1 MiB at Best and 0.25 MiB at Fast; the cap of 160 images never binds on a realistic stage (it needs about 4,000×2,000 CSS px); a 1,100–2,560 px stage holds about 27–59 MiB at Best (56–108 MiB with the trail) against 7–15 MiB (14–27) at Fast. Chromium may also limit the total memory of GPU-backed canvases (about 256 MB, from memory, unverified), so compare fps after a long drive, not only at the start.
- **Not defects** (so a QA doesn't file them): flat tile colours for 0.3–0.7 s at Best (0.15–0.3 s at Fast) after a load, a teleport or a switch; one to three extra frames after a pause, a load or a switch (the limit's formula catches `next` up to `ts`, `src/surface.js:445`, inside the test's ±1); the readout wobbling 59–61 at a limit of 60 on a 144 Hz screen; a 50 ms frame every 10–15 s at a limit of 30 on a 59.94 or 60.06 Hz screen; a global storm having 330 grains at Best (220 before). The `dt = 1 / 60` default of `draw()` (`src/surface.js:1002`) is dead code, because `loop()` is its only caller and always passes `dt`.
