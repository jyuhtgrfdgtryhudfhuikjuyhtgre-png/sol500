# Changelog

Each milestone was built from a written design spec and plan, kept in [`docs/superpowers/`](docs/superpowers/).

## 3.8.7 — 2026-11-12
- "Plan a mission" is on the title screen.
- The backpack and rover cargo slots have labels.
- The Kestrel Survey (the robots sent before you) is explained.
- How to play shows a picture for each of its 12 steps; four are real game screens.

## 3.8.6 — 2026-11-12
- Fixes from release QA: a Showcase card waits for an open dialog, and the launch round counts its science.

## 3.8.5 — 2026-11-12
- Showcase cards carry pictures and numbers.
- Ice, the river delta, region borders and the fog's edge are drawn round and soft, not stepped.
- Room for the pressurized rover's battery bar; four astronauts sit two by two.

## 3.8.4 — 2026-11-12
- The Showcase opens with the game's purpose, and the physics engine is its first card.
- Two dashed bars on the ground show where a moving rover would stop on Earth and on Mars.
- The story card names Apollo 11, Artemis, Opportunity and Perseverance.
- Crewmates' name tags show only when they matter; Showcase scenes use the full width.

## 3.8.3 — 2026-11-12
- Bigger base buildings, and more land at every base rank.
- The parked rover is the model you drive.

## 3.8.2 — 2026-11-11
- In co-op, your teammates see the tool you are working with.

## 3.8.1 — 2026-11-11
- The astronaut comes alive: a low-gravity hop, footstep dust, breathing, a visor glint and a red pulse on low air.
- A tool for each job, animated: pick, drill, scanner, crowbar, wrench and medkit.

## 3.8.0 — 2026-11-11 · Base ranks and vehicles (M6)
- Base ranks Camp → Outpost → Station → Colony, each chosen by the player and paid for in materials; each grows the land and unlocks buildings.
- The Garage builds a pressurized rover (breathe inside, no suit drain) and a scout drone (based on Ingenuity) that clears the fog.
- The Fabricator makes bigger suit tanks, a bigger backpack, rover cargo racks and faster tools.
- The Colony's launch pad sends samples home.

## 3.7.0 — 2026-11-10 · Polish, Quick mission and Showcase (M5)
- A short Quick mission, and a Showcase gallery whose cards jump straight into the real game.

## 3.6.0 — 2026-11-09 · Progression and team (M4d)
- The rover is driven by a physics engine: Newton's laws with Mars gravity.
- The ground is painted from NASA photos; a field guide, Science notes and the "Why Mars?" story.
- Relay beacons, expedition goals, role skills, pings, and an Expedition section in the debrief.

## 3.5.0 — 2026-10-27 · Points of interest (M4c)
- Landmarks with discovery cards, Kestrel Survey structures and natural formations.

## 3.4.0 — 2026-10-16 · Dangers (M4b)
- Solar flares and shelters, caves, dust devils, dust-storm visibility and display settings.

## 3.3.0 — 2026-10-06 · Expedition core (M4a)
- Finds to collect, a backpack, suit oxygen and the rover's battery.

## 3.2.0 — 2026-10-04 · Endless world (M3)
- An endless Mars to explore, with one astronaut per player.

## 3.1.0 — 2026-10-01 · Admin panel (M2)
- A host's admin panel for rooms and mission history.

## 3.0.0 — 2026-10-01 · Desktop app and online play (M1)
- A Windows app, and a share link so friends can join from anywhere.

## Before 3.0 — 2026-09-30
- The simulation engine and balance bots, crew roles, building, revives, Earth support, difficulty levels, up to 5 crew, English and Vietnamese text, a playable web game with LAN co-op, charts and art, and a walkable outpost.
